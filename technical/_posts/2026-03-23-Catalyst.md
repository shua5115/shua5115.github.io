*Presenting Catalyst Mobile Base in the CMU Bakery Square Lab*

I am in a team of 5 students working with the [CMU Cloud Lab](https://the-tartan.org/2024/02/19/cmu-cloud-lab-to-bring-remote-science-into-mainstream/)
to develop a robotic system that completes lab experiments automatically.
The concept we have designed transports lab materials between automated storage and lab instruments,
such as a [liquid handler](https://www.hamiltoncompany.com/automated-liquid-handling), closing the loop
for common experiments involving small fluid volumes.
This project is part of the [MRSD Program](https://mrsd.ri.cmu.edu/) at CMU as an
exercise in mechatronics, computer vision, systems engineering, and project management.

Covered in demo:
1. Base subsystem
    - Safety stop with current sensing
    - Autonomous navigation with Nav2 stack
    - Collision avoidance with Lidar sensing
    - Navigation accuracy within 10 cm
2. Arm subsystem
    - Computer-vision safety check
    - Picking from onboard stand and placing into liquid handler, using force feedback localization
    - Picking from liquid handler and placing into onboard stand, using hybrid admittance control
    - Collision avoidance with real-time 3D map

## Notable Contributions

- Integrated software dependencies with ROS 2 Humble in Python and C++
- Improved navigation stack accuracy and reliability to meet system requirements of 10 cm accuracy and obstacle avoidance
- Programmed serial comms with RobotEQ motor driver to be robust to failure
- Designed mechanical structure and mechanisms of gripper parts and frame
- Supported developing arm team's picking/placing strategy
- Learned MicroBasic scripting to fix RobotEQ motor driver functionality
