In F1Tenth, I learn how to apply a wide range of robotics techniques to win in RC car races.
- State space planning for obstacle avoidance
- Optimal planning for taking the best racing line
- Sensor fusion of odometry and LiDAR for accurate localization
- C++ code optimization for constrained hardware
