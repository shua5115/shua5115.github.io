I am in a team of 5 students working with the [CMU Cloud Lab](https://the-tartan.org/2024/02/19/cmu-cloud-lab-to-bring-remote-science-into-mainstream/)
to develop a robotic system that completes lab experiments automatically.
The concept we have designed transports lab materials between automated storage and lab instruments,
such as a [liquid handler](https://www.hamiltoncompany.com/automated-liquid-handling), closing the loop
for common experiments involving small fluid volumes.
This project is part of the [MRSD Program](https://mrsd.ri.cmu.edu/) at CMU as an
exercise in mechatronics, computer vision, systems engineering, and project management.

## Responsibilities

- Stakeholder communication
- Designing mechatronic systems
- Integrating systems
- Testing systems
- Co-lead of mobile robot base
- Co-lead of navigation stack
- Project manager (interim)
- Manufacturing mechanical parts
